Online voice is an unofficial free endpoint: needs internet, may stop working, and sends the message text to Google. Installing the Windows voice is the reliable fix.

Speech comes out of this device's audio output. Pair your Bluetooth speaker (or plug the AUX cable) to this device, then press Speak.

Daily schedule

Runs only while this page stays open on this device. Browsers need one tap on the page first to allow sound, so press Speak or Start once after opening.

Alert tones

Generated in the browser, no audio files needed.

Keep device awake

Stops the screen from sleeping so announcements keep working. On the shop device, use "Add to Home Screen" and leave it plugged in.

Live camera preview

Camera needs https (Vercel gives you that). Localhost also works.

External stream (optional)

Browsers cannot play the DVR's RTSP address directly. Convert it first (MediaMTX or ffmpeg on a Pi or laptop gives you an HLS link), then paste that link here.

Receive mode (same device / same browser)

Open this page in two tabs: one sends, the other (receive on) plays. This links tabs on one device only. Sending between different phones needs a small server, which this static page does not have.
